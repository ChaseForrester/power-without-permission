[ THE MANIFESTO ]
"I stopped waiting to be picked. So did every client who's hit their first 10K month with me."

My journey started in network marketing, as a mum who just needed to build an income for my son, who has a disability. There was no five-year plan — I simply needed to find a way to provide for him, on my own terms, without waiting for someone to hand me permission to do it.

That need turned into a business, and that business turned into a personal brand. Along the way I've grown into a law student, a speaker, and the owner and host of my own podcast — not because I followed a traditional path, but because I built the one that actually worked for my life.

I'm also a survivor of sexual assault. That experience is part of why I built what I've built — I know what it feels like to have power taken from you, and I know what it takes to claim it back. It's shaped how I show up as a coach, a speaker, and an advocate for people rebuilding on their own terms.

Power Without Permission is that philosophy in one line: you don't need the perfect qualification, the perfect timing, or someone else's approval to start. You need to move. That's what I help my clients do — turn what they already have into consistent, unstoppable growth.
